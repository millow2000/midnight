Midnight Tales
  Original English suspense short stories. Free to read, no signup, no paywall.
Midnight Tales is a personal literary site publishing original suspense and supernatural short fiction in English. Every story is free to read in full online.

Stories
- Human Skin — Seven years into her marriage, heavy with child, she went to catch her husband cheating. She pushed the door open and found a floor of blood and two flayed human skins. One belonged to the other woman. The other belonged to her husband. Later she discovered that the whole world had been his betrothal gift to her.
- Mirror Abyss — When I woke, I had become a palace maid of ancient times—while my missing boyfriend was already another woman’s husband. The emperor here loathes time travelers; every three years comes a purge. I pretended to drown in imperial favor; he pretended to give me his true heart. On the night of the Mid-Autumn Festival, I discovered he had seen through everything long ago.
- The Dwarf — She tasted love for the first time—and for the first time saw the true shape of hatred. I will walk through hell beside you, then go and see heaven in your place.
- Cut Out The Heart — She was the disciple her master trusted the least. He stripped away her spiritual powers and cast her into the forbidden grounds—yet in that desperate place she met an imprisoned dragon. Seven days tangled together, and she never knew she had carried off a dragon's heart.
- The Collar — A fierce dog that tore off a child's arm vanished overnight. And with it disappeared the woman whom half the neighborhood had wished dead. But strangely, no one heard the dog bark.
- The Nine-Tailed Fox Clan — Her sister was dead. She drew her sword, descended the mountain, and killed the murderer with a single strike. Then she discovered that the people in the lowlands were far more terrifying than any demon.
- Ghost Car — A late-night last bus announces its destination as "Heaven" — and the passenger who gets off is instantly decapitated.
- The Three Taboos My Grandfather Left Me — Some rides, once boarded, you can never get off. Some taboos, once broken, stay with you for the rest of your life.
- Who Gets the Deer — I thought that with proof of her murder in my grasp, I could tame her into my cash cow — until the day she smiled and asked me: did that man really die?
- Ghost Money — I am a courier who delivers parcels between the worlds of the living and the dead. On the seventh night after her death, I knocked on a family's door to reclaim five notes of ghost money.
- Honey Glazed Char Siu — He thought he had caught the freshest prey, little knowing that in this hunt, who was the hunter and who was the hunted remained to be seen.
- The Flying Fish's Scheme — When the flying fish draws its blade, blood is sure to flow. Everyone says the Flying Fish of Yandang is one person — but few know it is a pair of brothers.
- After One Hundred Thousand Sword Strokes — Ning Family Fortress burned to ash overnight, and the young Ning Wanjian knelt in the snow seeking only a sword that could avenge his family.
