# Midnight Tales

> Original English suspense short stories. Free to read, no signup, no paywall.

Midnight Tales is a personal literary site publishing original suspense and supernatural short fiction in English. Every story is free to read in full online.

- Website: https://greenstonenovel.com/
- Author: ziye
- Language: English
- License: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/)

## Stories

- [The Collar](https://greenstonenovel.com/en/collar.html) — A fierce dog that tore off a child's arm vanished overnight. And with it disappeared the woman whom half the neighborhood had wished dead. But strangely, no one heard the dog bark.
- [The Nine-Tailed Fox Clan](https://greenstonenovel.com/en/ninetailedfox.html) — Her sister was dead. She drew her sword, descended the mountain, and killed the murderer with a single strike. Then she discovered that the people in the lowlands were far more terrifying than any demon.
- [Ghost Car](https://greenstonenovel.com/en/ghostcar.html) — A late-night last bus announces its destination as "Heaven" — and the passenger who gets off is instantly decapitated.
- [The Three Taboos My Grandfather Left Me](https://greenstonenovel.com/en/threetaboos.html) — Some rides, once boarded, you can never get off. Some taboos, once broken, stay with you for the rest of your life.
- [Who Gets the Deer](https://greenstonenovel.com/en/whogetsthedeer.html) — I thought that with proof of her murder in my grasp, I could tame her into my cash cow — until the day she smiled and asked me: did that man really die?
- [Ghost Money](https://greenstonenovel.com/en/mingbi.html) — I am a courier who delivers parcels between the worlds of the living and the dead. On the seventh night after her death, I knocked on a family's door to reclaim five notes of ghost money.
- [Honey Glazed Char Siu](https://greenstonenovel.com/en/mizhichashao.html) — He thought he had caught the freshest prey, little knowing that in this hunt, who was the hunter and who was the hunted remained to be seen.
- [The Flying Fish's Scheme](https://greenstonenovel.com/en/feiyu.html) — When the flying fish draws its blade, blood is sure to flow. Everyone says the Flying Fish of Yandang is one person — but few know it is a pair of brothers.
- [After One Hundred Thousand Sword Strokes](https://greenstonenovel.com/en/huijian.html) — Ning Family Fortress burned to ash overnight, and the young Ning Wanjian knelt in the snow seeking only a sword that could avenge his family.

All stories are complete, free to read in the browser, and require no registration.
