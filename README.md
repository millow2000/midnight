What this is
Midnight Tales is a personal literary site publishing Chinese suspense and folk-horror short fiction in two sections:
- Suspense Stories — original short stories written for the site.
- Classics of the Strange — modern English retellings of public-domain Chinese classical zhiguai / guai-tan literature, drawn from Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio (Liaozhai zhiyi) by Pu Songling.
Not detective fiction. Not serial killers. Not psychological thrillers about a haunted Victorian mansion. The dread here runs on older machinery:
- folk taboos that must never be broken
- village rites and offerings to restless ancestors
- hungry ghosts, and paper money burned for the dead
- karmic debts repaid blade by blade
- imperial palaces, and wuxia blades avenging burned fortresses
It lives in a mountain village where the rain brings wedding guests who are not people. In a rice jar that must stay closed. In a night bus whose destination reads "Heaven".
Every work is free to read in full, in the browser. No signup, no paywall.
